Aviora For Schools

A classroom without four walls.

A school visit to Aviora gives students the opportunity to learn directly from nature. Instead of simply reading about birds, they can observe real behaviors, explore habitats and ask questions alongside experienced educators.

Our programs are designed to complement classroom learning while encouraging curiosity, empathy, environmental responsibility and a deeper appreciation for the natural world.

School Visit FAQ

Questions teachers ask.

Here are a few helpful answers before you bring your students to Aviora.

We recommend contacting our education team as early as possible, especially for larger groups or preferred dates during busy school seasons.

Our programs can be adapted for primary, middle and secondary students. We can also create custom sessions for clubs and youth organizations.

Yes. Our education team can discuss your learning objectives and suggest activities around topics such as biology, ecology, biodiversity, habitats and conservation.

Absolutely. Schools can combine guided walks, workshops, bird encounters and conservation activities to create a schedule that fits their group.

Photography may be included as part of selected programs. Our educators will explain the appropriate ways to photograph birds without disturbing them.

Comfortable outdoor clothing, suitable footwear, drinking water and any school-approved learning materials are recommended. We'll provide additional visit information before your arrival.
