Life At Aviora

More than beautiful birds.
Living stories.

Aviora is home to a diverse collection of remarkable birds. Some were born here, while others arrived after rescue, rehabilitation or circumstances that made life in the wild impossible.

Our goal is simple: create a safe, enriching environment where every bird can thrive while helping visitors understand the importance of protecting wild habitats.

Meet birds from forests, wetlands, mountains and tropical ecosystems.
Learn about their natural behaviors, diets and habitats.
Discover how responsible conservation can protect their future.

Protecting Their Future

Every encounter can inspire conservation.

Meeting birds is only the beginning. At Aviora, every experience is designed to help visitors understand how individual choices and conservation work can make a difference for wild bird populations.

Rescue & Rehabilitation

Providing care and a safe environment for birds that need long-term support.

Education

Helping visitors understand birds, ecosystems and responsible wildlife experiences.

Habitat Protection

Supporting awareness around forests, wetlands and other habitats birds depend upon.

Responsible Encounters

Creating respectful experiences that put bird welfare first.
